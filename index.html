ABOUT PERIDOT

A place to pause, heal and begin again.

Founded over Loading... years ago, Peridot Foundation is a recovery centre in Accra dedicated to holistic rehabilitation and long-term recovery.

At Peridot foundations, our team works to create a structured environment focused on healing the mind, body and spirit. Our clinical and support team includes a Medical Doctor, professional Nurse, Counsellor, Anger Management Counsellor, Gym Instructor, Swimming Instructor and Sports Instructors for football and basketball, alongside 24/7 on-site security for residents.

Our mission

Support people recovering from substance use, helping them rediscover their potential and reconnect with their community.

Our vision

Set a standard for compassionate care by combining heart-led treatment, modern research and inspiring education.

WHAT WE OFFER

Support built around the whole person.

Recovery is more than stopping a substance. Our programme combines therapeutic, social, family and physical support.

01

12-Step Recovery

A structured programme supporting accountability, personal growth and a foundation for lasting recovery.

02

Psychotherapy & Counselling

Individual counselling and therapeutic support to explore challenges, emotions and healthier coping strategies.

03

Family Support

Family counselling, Al-Anon and Nar-Anon support help families understand recovery and build healthier relationships.

04

Relapse Prevention

Learn to identify high-risk situations, manage triggers and develop practical coping strategies for life after treatment.

05

Anger & Depression Support

Specialised counselling focused on emotional regulation, healthy coping mechanisms and mental wellbeing.

06

Aftercare Planning

A personalised roadmap can include outpatient check-ins and connections with ongoing recovery support.

07

Social-Model Recovery

A supportive community environment that encourages responsibility, connection, discipline and positive routines.

08

Bilingual & Multilingual Support

Counselling and recovery support are available in English, French, Ewe and Twi.

OUR APPROACH

Recovery with structure, dignity and human connection.

We combine holistic methods with evidence-informed practice and a social model of recovery. The goal is to help each person develop the understanding, routines and support network needed beyond treatment.
